# News & Highlights

Welcome to my news and highlights page! Here, I share updates on my academic journey, teaching activities, research publications, and professional achievements.

---

### November 2026 | Teaching Microprocessor I at Oklahoma State University

I am currently serving as an **Adjunct Assistant Professor** in the School of Materials, Mechatronics and Manufacturing Engineering at Oklahoma State University.

This semester, I am teaching **Microprocessor I (EET 3253)**, focusing on microcontroller programming, embedded systems, and hands-on learning. I have introduced practical hardware exercises and a final project in which students develop a programmable automatic light controller.

---

### August 2026 | Completed My Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering

I earned my **Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering** from **Oklahoma State University**.

My doctoral research focused on cooperative driving between human-driven and autonomous vehicles, with particular emphasis on control systems, optimization, human driving behavior, and experimental validation.

I am grateful to my research collaborators, mentors, and colleagues for their support throughout this journey.

---

### 2026 | Journal Paper Accepted in IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology

Our paper, **“Personalized and Adaptive Driver Advising for Cooperative Driving in Mixed Traffic,”** has been accepted for publication in *IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology*.

The research investigates personalized driving advisory strategies to support cooperation between human-driven and autonomous vehicles in mixed traffic environments.

**Authors:** J. Lu, S. Hossain, W. Sheng, and H. Bai.

### 2026 | Research Published in IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology

Our research paper, **“A Multi-Vehicle, Multi-Lane Cooperative Driving Framework in Mixed Traffic with Driving Advisory,”** was published in *IEEE Transactions on Vehicular Technology*.

The study explores cooperative driving strategies involving multiple vehicles across multiple lanes, with driving advisory approaches for improving coordination in mixed traffic.
