## About Me

I am currently an **Adjunct Assistant Professor** in the Department of Materials, Mechatronics and Manufacturing Engineering at Oklahoma State University, and I will be joining the department as a **full-time Visiting Assistant Professor in January 2027**.

I teach undergraduate engineering courses, with responsibilities in curriculum development, course management, student mentorship, and assessment design. Currently, I am teaching **Microprocessor I**, where I incorporate microcontroller programming, hands-on laboratory exercises, and engineering design projects to help students connect theoretical concepts with practical applications.

I earned my **Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from Oklahoma State University** and have 10 years of teaching experience and 9 years of research experience. My research focuses on autonomous vehicles, control systems, optimization, and experimental investigations of cooperative driving between human-driven and autonomous vehicles. I also have industry experience as a **Controls and Automation Engineer at Intralox LLC**.

I am passionate about engineering education and helping students develop critical thinking, practical engineering skills, and independent problem-solving abilities through hands-on learning and design projects.
